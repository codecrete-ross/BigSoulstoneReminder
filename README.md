# Big Soulstone Reminder

![Big Soulstone Reminder](logo.png)

A simple Soulstone reminder for Warlocks in World of Warcraft Retail and World of Warcraft: Forever.

## Features

- Open settings with `/bsr`
- Shows a reminder when Soulstone is missing
- The reminder goes away when your Soulstone is active on you or a party or raid member
- Choose whether the reminder appears while solo, in parties, in raids, or in open-world groups
- Choose when "Ready Soon" appears and where the banner sits on screen
- Preview the banner from the settings panel
- Standalone and lightweight

## How To Use

1. Install the addon and log onto a Warlock
2. Type `/bsr` to open the settings panel
3. In Retail, cast **Soulstone**. In Forever, cast **Create Soulstone**, then use the conjured stone from your bags.
4. Apply Soulstone to yourself or a current party or raid member to clear the reminder
5. Turn on open-world group reminders in settings if you want party and raid reminders outside instances

## Install

Install through CurseForge for your game version, or copy the `BigSoulstoneReminder` folder into the corresponding AddOns directory. [Big Soulstone Reminder on CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/bigsoulstonereminder)

| Client | Directory inside World of Warcraft |
| --- | --- |
| Retail | `_retail_/Interface/AddOns/` |
| Forever | `_forever_/Interface/AddOns/` |
